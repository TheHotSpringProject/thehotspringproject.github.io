Privacy Policy

The Hot Spring Project (website and Android app) · Effective November 15, 2026

The Hot Spring Project is a map of thermal springs in the United States. We don't ask you to create an account, and we don't collect, sell or share personal information. This page explains what the app does with the little data it touches.

Your location

If you tap the locate button or sort springs by distance, the app asks your device for your location. It's used on your device only, to center the map and to calculate distances to springs. It isn't sent to us or stored. You can deny or revoke location permission at any time in your device or browser settings, and the rest of the app keeps working.

Saved springs, visited springs and settings

Springs you save or mark as visited, plus preferences such as your chosen basemap and °F/°C, are stored only on your device (in the browser's local storage). They aren't uploaded anywhere. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling removes them.

Offline copies

To work without a signal, the app keeps copies of its own files, the spring list, and map tiles and Wikipedia content you have viewed, in your device's cache. These copies contain no personal information.

Third-party services

To show maps, photos and descriptions, your device requests content directly from these services, which receive standard request information (such as your IP address and the map area or place being shown) under their own privacy policies:

  • Map tiles: U.S. Geological Survey (The National Map), Esri, OpenTopoMap and OpenStreetMap.
  • Descriptions and photos: Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons (privacy policy).
  • Place photos: Google Maps Platform (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Hosting: GitHub Pages (GitHub Privacy Statement).

The app has no advertising, analytics or tracking code of its own.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions? Open an issue at github.com/TheHotSpringProject/thehotspringproject.github.io/issues.
